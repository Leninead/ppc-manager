### POST 1 — Coverage Gap
**Pilar:** Datos reales + sistema
**SVG:** `linkedin_post1_ppc.svg`
**Publicar:** Martes, 10 AM ART

**Dato clave:** 506 search terms con demanda real de compra, 8.1M búsquedas mensuales, 0% brand share.

---

$7,003 in ad spend.
1,157 search terms triggered.

Here's the split:

1,005 search terms → $3,652 spent → 0 orders.
152 search terms → $3,351 spent → 293 orders.

The account was spending almost equally on what worked and what didn't.

The difference: nobody had systematically identified which was which.

"women bladder control pads" — $139 spent. 12 clicks. 0 orders.
"adult diapers xxl" — $49 spent. 6 clicks. 0 orders.
"urinary pads for women" — $45 spent. 4 clicks. 0 orders.

These aren't random misses. They're patterns.
Terms that look relevant but don't convert for this specific product.

The system flagged 1,005 of them. Automatically.
Classified by priority. Ready to add as negatives.

You don't fix a 52% waste problem by adjusting bids.
You fix it by knowing exactly where the money is going.

### POST 3 — Portfolio ASIN
**Pilar:** Amazon PPC / Seller thinking
**SVG:** `linkedin_post3_portfolio.svg`
**Publicar:** Martes siguiente, 10 AM ART

**Dato clave:** Mismo presupuesto, 9 ASINs, ACoS de 33% a 363%. 2 ASINs consumiendo $223, devolviendo $94. 3 ASINs consumiendo $509, devolviendo $1,087.

---

Same account. Same budget. 9 products.

ACoS range: 33% to 363%.

That's not a campaign problem.
That's a portfolio problem.

2 ASINs were consuming $223 in ad spend and returning $94 in sales.
ACoS: 202% and 363%.

3 ASINs were consuming $509 in ad spend and returning $1,087 in sales.
ACoS: 33%, 48%, 48%.

Every dollar wasted on the bottom two
was a dollar not going to the top three.

The fix wasn't to optimize bids across the board.
It was to read the portfolio first.

Which ASINs have conversion rate above 12%? Fund those.
Which ones are below 5% with ACoS over 150%? Cut or restructure.

That decision used to take me an afternoon of spreadsheet work.
Now it takes one report and 10 minutes of thinking.

The thinking is still mine.
The spreadsheet work is gone.

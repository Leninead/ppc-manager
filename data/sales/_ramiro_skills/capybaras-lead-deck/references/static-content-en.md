## Slide 7 — Tattoo Care

**Brand:** Tattoo Care

**CHALLENGE:**
> Launch and development of the product portfolio in the American market for the Tattoo Care brand, specializing in pre- and post-tattoo skin care. After starting out in Argentina and Chile, they sought to expand and develop in the United States.

**SOLUTIONS & SERVICES:**
> During the setup phase, comprehensive work was done on account opening and planning (brand registration, stock and investment plan, incident management), distinctive branding (brand and packaging adaptation, listing development, A+, and storefront), and advertising (campaign strategy, management, and optimization) to improve organic sales rankings. In a second phase, with an established foundation, sales were scaled with the launch of two new products, and five additional products are being developed for this year. Additionally, work is underway to increase the brand's audience and traffic in the US with a Shopify store and social media content.

**RESULTS:**
- **5°** — Top ranking for the main keywords for an afterkit.
- **Amazon Choice** — badge obtained in 6 months. 4.5 Rating
- **6 Figure Sales** — obtained in 1st year with single product.

## Slide 8 — Wamery

**Brand:** Wamery

**CHALLENGE:**
> Improve conversion rates for Wamery, a well-established brand of water filters and kitchen items. The goal was to optimize Amazon advertising investment and overall account profitability.

**SOLUTIONS & SERVICES:**
> Two strategies were defined. On the one hand, a new, simpler and more accessible visual identity was created, which served as the basis for updating the main image, as well as the listings, A+, and the storefront. On the other hand, work was done to maximize external traffic to the listings by developing a social media content strategy and advertising campaigns on Google and Facebook.

**RESULTS:**
- **20%** — Increase in CTR after changing the Main Image.
- **50%** — Increased CR through the redesign of publications on Amazon.
- **-10%** — TACOS Reduction. For improved conversion and off-Amazon traffic strategy.

## Slide 9 — Shapermint

**Brand:** Shapermint

**CHALLENGE:**
> Scaling the annual sales of a leading women's apparel brand, which had been working with automated advertising campaign optimization software without adaptation at the product or target level. They also wanted weekly reporting and monitoring, as well as strategic recommendations, which they didn't have.

**SOLUTIONS & SERVICES:**
> A granular campaign process and structure was established, activating Sponsored Products, Sponsored Displays, and Sponsored Brands based on each product's main keywords and target audience. This way, we boosted organic sales in the "Best Sellers Rank" and were more efficient with our investment. We analyzed prices, ratings, rankings, and competition to propose coupons or adjustments to listings and prices. We also worked together on ad hoc strategies for new launches and special dates.

**RESULTS:**
- **#1°** — Top Ranking in its category.
- **61%** — Sales Increase. 4Q 23 vs 4Q 22.
- **-19%** — TACoS Reduction (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) PPC.

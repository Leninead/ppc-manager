Overview

Executive Summary

Safeko is sitting on 75 years of category authority as "The HairNet Company" but its digital footprint doesn't reflect it. The brand has a credible Amazon presence — multiple ASINs ranking on best-seller pages for commercial protective hairnets — yet the Shopify site itself is invisible for category keywords, runs zero paid social, has a near-empty social media stack, and shows backordered flags on its flagship SKUs at the moment of capture. The core gap is a missing top-of-funnel demand engine: competitors like Bison Life are operating on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn while Safeko relies almost entirely on B2B distribution plus organic Amazon. The opportunity for Capybaras is to convert Safeko's manufacturing depth into a full-funnel digital play — own the Amazon category, layer in Meta paid social to a younger DTC buyer, and clean up the Shopify experience so it stops leaking trust.

Detailed Analysis

Lane Breakdown

C
SEO & ORGANIC SEARCH
What's Working
Safeko owns the #1 position for its branded search term — no negative results or competitors hijacking the SERP.
The brand operates a blog at /blogs/hairnets with at least 4 published articles around food safety, compliance and use cases — a base layer of topical content exists.
Site is fully indexed across collections, individual products, contact and policy pages — no major crawlability blockers spotted.
Opportunities
The Shopify site does not appear on page 1 (or anywhere visible) for high-intent terms like "hair nets food service workers," "bulk hairnets" or "disposable beard nets" — Amazon listings show up but safeko.com itself is invisible.
Homepage title tag reads "Safeko – SAFEKO" and meta description is the placeholder phrase "Hairnets gloves and more PPE" — zero keyword targeting at the most valuable page.
Collection pages (Hairnets, Gloves, Face Masks) inherit the same generic meta — no unique title/description per category to capture commercial-intent traffic.
Blog cadence appears low and articles aren't optimized for search — clear opening to build a topical hub around "food safety compliance," "OSHA hairnet requirements," "GFSI / HACCP PPE."
F
PAID ADVERTISING
Opportunities
No active Meta ads detected — Safeko has no Instagram or Facebook business page linked anywhere on its site, so the paid social funnel doesn't exist at all today.
Foodservice managers, kitchen staff buyers, food-safety supervisors and salon owners are highly reachable on Meta — Safeko is letting that demand pool sit untouched.
Amazon Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brand campaigns should be running given the strong organic visibility on the marketplace — worth confirming inside Seller Central whether they're being managed.
Google Ads is the obvious second lever — competitors aren't dominating it either, so there's headroom to own "buy hairnets bulk," "wholesale PPE foodservice" and similar B2B search terms cheaply.
D
WEBSITE QUALITY
What's Working
HTTPS enforced, mobile viewport set, Shopify infrastructure is fast and modern — the technical foundation is solid.
Clear trust signals above the fold and in the footer: FDA Compliant Products, Same/Next Day Shipping, Hassle-Free Returns, 75 years of category heritage.
Dedicated B2B portal is exposed in the main nav — appropriate for the wholesale audience Safeko depends on.
Opportunities
Multiple flagship SKUs are flagged "backordered" — including the Lightweight Hairnet, Dispense-O-Net starter kit, Nitrile Examination Glove and Shoe Cover. These are the hero products. Visible stock-outs kill conversion and ad spend efficiency.
No customer reviews or ratings visible on product pages — a major trust gap in a category where buyers want proof before bulk ordering.
No email capture pop-up, exit intent, or B2B lead magnet (e.g. "Bulk Pricing Sheet"). Site traffic is leaving without being captured into a list.
No urgency, promo, or sale element anywhere on the homepage — flat presentation despite a competitive category.
D
SOCIAL PRESENCE
What's Working
LinkedIn page is set up and linked from the website footer — appropriate B2B channel given the foodservice/manufacturing buyer profile.
Opportunities
No Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or YouTube presence linked from the site — comparable competitors run all of these to nurture trust and educate buyers.
Bison Life maintains an active Instagram (~1.2K followers, 730+ posts), Facebook page and X/Twitter — Safeko is invisible in the channels where line cooks, kitchen managers and food-safety auditors actually scroll.
The Dispense-O-Net product is a visual, demo-friendly story tailor-made for short-form video (TikTok / Reels) — a clear missed organic content lever.
LinkedIn activity cadence should be verified — even if the page exists, dormant pages send a worse signal to B2B buyers than no page at all.
D
COMPETITIVE POSITION
What's Working
On Amazon specifically, Safeko punches above its weight — multiple ASINs (B0B6GHXH9J, B0B6GKZWWC, B0B6GFV1JJ and more) appearing in the Best Sellers list for Commercial Protective Hairnets, alongside category leaders like LEOBRO.
75-year heritage as "The HairNet Company" is a defensible authority moat that none of the cheap Amazon competitors can claim — but it's barely surfaced in current digital assets.
Opportunities
Bison Life is operating a multi-brand portfolio (Safe Handler, Kleen Handler, Kleen Chef, etc.) across organic, paid, social and Amazon — Safeko is competing single-brand against a portfolio play.
Safeko trails or matches competitors in 4 of 5 lanes (SEO, Paid, Website, Social). The Amazon strength is real but not insulating the brand from a digital pincer move by better-funded rivals.
Heritage story ("75 years as The HairNet Company") is the single biggest unused asset — needs to live on the homepage, ad creative, Amazon A+ Content and social bio.
Audit Findings

Website Issues Detected

CRITICAL
Flagship products showing "Backordered"
Lightweight Hairnet, Dispense-O-Net Starter Kit, Nitrile Examination Glove and Anti-Skid Shoe Cover all display backorder status on key variants. Fix: align inventory with the SKUs being driven by Amazon traffic, or hide unavailable variants until restocked rather than showing them as backordered on the live PDP.
Generic homepage title & meta description
Title is "Safeko – SAFEKO" and meta description is "Hairnets gloves and more PPE." Fix: rewrite to target commercial intent — e.g. "SAFEKO® | Hairnets, Beard Nets & Disposable PPE for Foodservice — Made by The HairNet Company."
MEDIUM
No product reviews surfaced on PDPs
Install a review app (Judge.me, Loox, Yotpo) and pull reviews directly from the Amazon listings via syndication — Safeko already has the social proof on Amazon, just needs to make it visible on Shopify.
No email capture or lead magnet
Add a pop-up or footer form offering bulk pricing, a foodservice compliance checklist, or first-order discount. Captures B2B leads that currently leave without trace.
Thin collection page metadata
Each collection (Hairnets, Beard Nets, Gloves, etc.) needs a unique title, meta description and 80–150 word intro paragraph targeting category keywords.
No social channels beyond LinkedIn
Set up Instagram and Facebook business profiles at minimum. Foodservice and manufacturing buyers research vendors on these platforms — absence creates a credibility gap.
LOW
Blog cadence and SEO optimization
Existing blog posts are useful but appear under-optimized. Add internal links to commercial collections, optimize H1/H2 structure, and publish at a steadier cadence (2x/month).
No urgency / promotional elements
Test a homepage banner for bulk-order discounts or seasonal compliance pushes (e.g. ahead of FDA inspections, back-to-school cafeteria season).
No live chat or buyer support widget
B2B buyers often need quick spec confirmations (size charts, case quantities, lead times). A simple chat widget or "Request a Quote" CTA on collection pages would meaningfully lift inquiry rate.
